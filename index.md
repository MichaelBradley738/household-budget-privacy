# Household Budget Dashboard — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 November 2026

## 1. About this application
Household Budget Dashboard is a private, non-commercial personal finance application being developed for household budgeting and financial management.

## 2. Financial information
With the account holder's authorisation, the application is intended to receive financial information through Openfeed, including:

- Bank account details and balances
- Transaction dates, descriptions and amounts
- Financial information needed for budgeting, reporting and savings tracking

The application may also process manually provided transaction statements from other financial providers.

## 3. How information is used
Financial information is intended to be used solely for personal budgeting, transaction categorisation, cash-flow analysis, savings tracking and financial reporting.

The application is not intended to sell personal information or use financial data for advertising.

## 4. Storage and security
Financial information will be stored in the application's configured data storage environment. Access will be restricted to authorised users, and appropriate measures will be implemented to protect financial information and authentication credentials.

Storage arrangements and security measures will be finalised before live financial information is imported.

## 5. Data sharing
Openfeed facilitates the authorised transfer of financial information from participating financial institutions to this application.

The application is not intended to share information with advertisers or sell financial information to third parties. Any additional service providers used for hosting or processing will be disclosed as they are introduced.

## 6. Retention and deletion
Financial information will be retained as needed for the application's budgeting purposes. Procedures for deleting stored financial information and associated credentials will be established before live operation.

Banking data-sharing consent may be withdrawn through the relevant Openfeed or banking controls. Revoking consent does not automatically delete information previously imported into the application.

## 7. Changes to this policy
This policy may be updated as the application's features, storage arrangements and data-processing practices develop.

## 8. Contact
Privacy enquiries can be directed to the application's owner through the contact method provided during application registration.
